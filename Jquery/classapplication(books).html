🧳Musafir Cafe

Musafir Café by Divya Prakash Dubey is a modern romance about Sudha, an ambitious divorce lawyer, and Chander, a techie with dreams of writing. Defying societal pressures, they choose an unconventional live-in relationship over marriage. The book explores urban struggles, personal freedom, and the balance between chasing individual dreams and nurturing love.

🍂October Junction

October Junction is a contemporary Hindi novel by Divya Prakash Dubey that explores an unconventional bond between two people, Chitra and Sudeep, who agree to meet for just one day a year over the span of a decade.

🪶Gunaho Ka Devta

Gunahon Ka Devta (The God of Sins) is a tragic 1949 Hindi novel by Dharamvir Bharati that explores the painful conflict between pure love, social duty, and caste boundaries in Allahabad. It centers on the unspoken romance between Chander, a bright student, and Sudha, his college professor's daughter.

📘Personality Plus

Explores four basic personality types - Sanguine, Choleric, Melancholy, and Phlegmatic ---- helping readers understand their own temperament and better interact with different personalities.
