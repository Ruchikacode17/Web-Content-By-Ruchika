💸UPI (Unified Payments Interface)

India's Unified Payments Interface "Most widely used payment method" UPI accounts for nearly 49% of the world's real-time digital payments, processing over 660 million transactions daily. Launched on April 11, 2016, it is the largest instant payment network globally. UPI Leads the World in Real Time Payments. India's Unified Payments Interface is also now the world's number one real-time payment system. It's the most widely used payment method in India, having even surpassed traditional cash transactions for everyday retail and merchant payments.
Instant Transfers: Transfers funds directly and immediately between bank accounts 24/7. Ease of Use: Operates through simple smartphone applications using QR code scanning or a unique UPI ID. Security: Protected by two-factor authentication and a secure personal UPI PIN.

🔒 DigiLocker

Government Official Digital Account Secure all you original id's DigiLocker is a secure, cloud-based platform launched by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) under India's Digital India program. It allows users to store, share, and verify authentic official documents and certificates that are legally recognized at par with originals.
Legal Validity: Documents fetched directly from issuers carry the same legal weight as original physical documents under the Information Technology Act, 2000. Accessibility: View and present important IDs like Aadhaar, PAN, driving licenses, and mark sheets anytime and anywhere via mobile app or web. User Consent: Secure sharing system via OAuth 2.0 protocol ensuring data is shared only with explicit user permission. Paperless Governance: Minimizes physical paperwork and streamlines verification for education, healthcare, and financial services.

🩺 eSanjeevani

eSanjeevani is India's flagship national telemedicine service and the world's largest government-owned primary healthcare teleconsultation platform. It has served around 250 million patients across the country entirely free of cost.
Digital Integration: Linked directly with the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) to generate ABHA IDs and maintain electronic health records. Accessibility: Generates instant digital e-prescriptions after a consultation, reducing travel expenses and overcrowding at secondary or tertiary hospitals.

📲 UMANG

UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance) "Combine Platform" UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance) is a key Digital India platform developed by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) and the National e-Governance Division (NeGD). It provides single-window access to thousands of central, state, and local government services.
Unified Access: Combines services from multiple ministries, departments, and states into one app. Multilingual Support: Operates in 23 Indian languages. Cross-Platform: Available on Android, iOS, and via the UMANG Web Portal. Integrated Services: Direct connection to utilities like DigiLocker and Aadhaar.
