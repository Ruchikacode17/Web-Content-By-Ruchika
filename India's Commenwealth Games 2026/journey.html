MORE THAN A CENTURY OF SPORT

A Journey Built on Determination

India's Commonwealth story is not simply about medals. It is about generations of athletes who carried the nation's hopes, competed with courage and created moments that became part of sporting history.

1934 — 2030

1934 THE BEGINNING

INDIA'S DEBUT

The First Step

India made its Commonwealth Games debut in 1934, marking the beginning of a sporting journey that would span generations.

Since that first appearance, Indian athletes have continued to compete across generations and disciplines.

1934
2010 DELHI

INDIA HOSTS

Delhi Takes Centre Stage

The 2010 Commonwealth Games brought the sporting world to New Delhi from 3 to 14 October.

India produced its biggest medal haul in Commonwealth Games history at that time, winning an incredible 101 medals.

101 MEDALS

2022 BIRMINGHAM

THE LEGACY CONTINUES

Birmingham 2022

Birmingham brought another chapter in India's Commonwealth story.

Indian athletes continued to shine across weightlifting, wrestling, boxing, badminton, table tennis and athletics.

THE LATEST CHAPTER

Glasgow 2026

Glasgow hosted the 2026 Commonwealth Games from 23 July to 2 August.

India finished fourth on the final medal table with a total of 39 medals.

2030 AMDAVAD

THE NEXT CHAPTER

Amdavad 2030

The Commonwealth Sport General Assembly formally confirmed Amdavad as host of the 2030 Centenary Commonwealth Games.

The Games will return to India twenty years after Delhi 2010, opening a new chapter for Commonwealth Sport.
