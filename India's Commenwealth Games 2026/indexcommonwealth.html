India's Commonwealth Story

From a historic debut in 1934 to a new generation of champions at Glasgow 2026.

More Than A Medal

India's Commonwealth journey is a story of determination, resilience and sporting ambition.

India first appeared at the Commonwealth Games in 1934. Since then, generations of Indian athletes have carried the nation's hopes onto the international stage.

At Glasgow 2026, India continued that legacy, finishing fourth on the medal table with 39 medals.
