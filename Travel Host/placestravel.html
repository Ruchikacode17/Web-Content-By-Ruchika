🌍 Top Tourist Choice

India ke kuch sabse beautiful aur must-visit destinations jaha nature, adventure aur peace sab kuch milta hai.

Ladakh

Ladakh apni surreal beauty aur high-altitude deserts ke liye jaana jata hai. Yahan ki Pangong Lake, Nubra Valley aur snow-covered mountains tourist ko ek alag hi duniya ka experience dete hain. Clear blue skies, peaceful monasteries aur rugged terrain is place ko India ka most breathtaking travel destination banate hain. Adventure lovers ke liye yeh trekking, biking aur camping ka paradise hai.

📅 Best Time: May – September

🏍 Activities: Bike Ride, Camping

Himachal

Himachal Pradesh apni lush green valleys, snow-capped peaks aur charming hill stations ke liye famous hai. Manali, Shimla aur Dharamshala jaise places yahan ki natural beauty ko aur bhi special banate hain. Yeh state nature lovers aur honeymoon couples ke liye perfect escape hai jahan har season ek naya scenic view deta hain.

📅 Best Time: March – June

🚗 Activities: Trekking, Camping

Uttarakhand

Uttarakhand ko “Devbhoomi” bhi kaha jata hai kyunki yeh spiritual aur natural beauty ka perfect blend hai. Kedarnath, Rishikesh aur Nainital jaise places yahan ko both pilgrimage aur adventure destination banate hain. Ganga river, Himalayan ranges aur peaceful environment is jagah ko soul-refreshing experience dete hain.

📅 Best Time: April – June

🛕 Activities: Temple Visit, Hiking
