THE POWER ABOVE US

One source. Endless potential.

Every day, the Sun delivers enormous amounts of energy to Earth. Solar technology allows us to capture a small part of that energy and turn it into electricity.

Unlike finite fossil fuels, sunlight is naturally replenished. That makes solar energy an important part of the transition toward a cleaner energy future.

Renewable by nature. Powered by a source that rises again every morning.
Turning sunlight into possibility.

FROM SUNLIGHT TO ELECTRICITY

How does solar energy actually work?

Solar panels do more than simply absorb sunlight. A series of connected steps transforms solar radiation into usable electrical power.

01

STEP ONE

Sunlight

Sunlight reaches the photovoltaic cells inside a solar panel.

02

STEP TWO

Solar Cells

Photovoltaic cells convert sunlight into direct current electricity.

03

STEP THREE

Inverter

An inverter converts the electricity into alternating current for everyday use.

04

STEP FOUR

Your Home

Clean electricity can now power lights, appliances and other devices.

Sunlight → Electricity → Everyday power

INSIDE THE TECHNOLOGY

What happens inside a panel?

Solar panels contain photovoltaic cells, commonly made using semiconductor materials. When sunlight reaches these cells, it helps create an electrical current.

Photovoltaic Cells

Capture energy from incoming sunlight.

Electrical Current

Converts captured solar energy into electricity.

Energy Conversion

THINK AGAIN

Solar myths, meet reality.

MYTH

Solar only works in very hot weather.

REALITY

Solar panels need sunlight rather than extreme heat. Their performance depends on factors such as sunlight intensity, orientation and system conditions.

MYTH

Solar energy is useful only for large buildings.

REALITY

Solar systems can be designed for different scales, from residential installations to large solar farms.

MYTH

Solar means you never need the grid.

REALITY

Grid-connected solar systems can work alongside the electricity grid, while some systems can also include batteries.

LOOKING AHEAD

Solar energy is evolving through improvements in efficiency, energy storage, smart systems and integration with wider electricity networks.

The bigger opportunity is not simply producing more energy. It is learning how to produce, store and use energy more intelligently.
