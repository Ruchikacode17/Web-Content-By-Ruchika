QUICK EXPERIMENTS

Think about energy differently.

Small observations can make invisible energy use easier to understand.

01

Switch Off

Look around your room and identify appliances or lights that are running without being actively used.

02

Check the Rating

Find the wattage label on an appliance and compare how much power different devices require.

03

Imagine Solar

Notice where sunlight reaches your home and think about how that space could support renewable energy.

04

Feel the Wind

Observe how moving air changes throughout the day and connect it to the basic idea behind wind power.
